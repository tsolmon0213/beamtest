BEAMxUFE Stock Investment Competition
Official rules and regulations
1. Overview
The BEAMxUFE Stock Investment Competition is a simulated trading challenge run jointly by the Business Education Association of Mongolia and UFE. Participants manage a virtual portfolio of 180,000,000 MNT over the competition period and are ranked by portfolio performance. The competition is for education and entertainment; no real money is invested or at risk.
2. Eligibility
Open to all high school and university students of Mongolia. Each participant may register only one team; duplicate or shared accounts result in disqualification of all BEAM associated activities and events.
3. Registration & Timeline
Registration will open [DATE] and close on [DATE]. Late entries are not accepted. Each participant starts with the same simulated starting balance. The competition run will run for four weeks of uninterrupted trading, starting September 21st 08:00am, and one week dedicated to writing the report. The competition will officially end on October 25th 23:59pm. 

On September 19th, {TIME}, a workshop will be held for all participants outlining and going through an in-depth tutorial on how to use StockTrak. Though not mandatory, this workshop may assist you and your team in navigating the program. 
4. Trading Rules
Eligible instruments are limited to the ones available on the simulator. All trades are final once executed. {EDIT & FINISH LATER}
5. Scoring & Rankings
Rankings are determined by three separate rubrics: Report, Stock Portfolio, and Quality of Trades. A more detailed version of how we determine top performing teams can be found here. We recommend everyone read the explicit criteria and rubric!
6. Prohibited Conduct
Any form of collusion, account sharing, exploiting simulator bugs, attempting to manipulate prices outside normal market activity, and submitting any content not written by the teams themselves for the report is strictly prohibited. Violations result in immediate disqualification and forfeiture of any prizes or standings.
7. Prizes & Recognition
Everyone will receive a participation certificate which will be distributed at our closing ceremony. Additionally, the top 3 performing teams will receive the following prizes:

1st place: IDAX account preloaded with 1,000,000 MNT, Gold medals, Trophy
2nd place: IDAX account preloaded with 500,000 MNT, Silver medals
3rd place: IDAX account preloaded with 250,000 MNT, Bronze medals

8. Disqualification & Disputes
BEAM and UFE organisers reserve the right to disqualify any participant found in violation of these rules and to make final decisions on disputed rankings. All decisions by the organizing committee are final.
9. Contact
Questions about registration, rules, or standings should be directed to either: beam.mongolia@gmail.com, or our Instagram @beam.mongolia, and we will reply to you as soon as we can!
